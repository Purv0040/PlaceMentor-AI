Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is AI Placement Copilot? ▼

AI Placement Copilot is an end-to-end engineering preparation platform that unifies your resume, code repositories, algorithmic history, and speech practice into a dynamic 90-day execution plan with continuous AI feedback.

Does this platform guarantee a placement or offer? ▼

No. We strictly do not provide artificial or misleading "job guarantees". Success depends on your disciplined practice and interview performance. Our role is to give you honest diagnostics, eliminate skill gaps, and optimize your study velocity.

Can I upload my existing resume and connect my accounts? ▼

Yes. You can upload any standard PDF resume. The system vectorizes your technical claims and cross-references them against your connected GitHub repositories and LeetCode solve history.

What happens if I miss a few days on the 90-day roadmap? ▼

The AI Adaptive Scheduling Engine automatically recalculates your sprint velocity. Incomplete tasks can be rescheduled with a single click, or absorbed into weekend practice blocks without derailing your campus timeline.

Can I switch my target role (e.g. from Backend to AI/ML)? ▼

Yes. You can update your primary target track anytime inside Settings. The system immediately re-evaluates your skill gaps and adjusts roadmap weights accordingly.
